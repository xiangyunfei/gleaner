# Privacy Policy

**Gleaner** — Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Gleaner accesses, how it is used and stored, and how you can remove it. Gleaner is a self-hosted, single-user personal tool run by its owner on the owner's own server.

## 1. Information we access

If you connect your Google account, Gleaner requests this read-only scope:

- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly` — to read the list of channels you are subscribed to (channel ID, channel title and thumbnail).

Gleaner does not request any other Google permission and does not access your email, contacts, files, comments, playlists, watch history or any other Google data.

## 2. How we use it

The subscription list is used only to show you the channels you follow and to let you choose new videos from them. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any purpose unrelated to this feature.

Videos you choose are processed on the server you run Gleaner on: the audio is transcribed with open-source speech recognition and organized into an article. Transcripts and articles are generated from the content of the videos you select and are kept for your own personal use.

## 3. How we store it

- The OAuth refresh token and the cached subscription list are stored only on the server where you run Gleaner, in a protected local location. They are never uploaded to any third-party service.
- Videos, transcripts and articles are stored only on that same server.
- Gleaner does not have a central service and does not collect data on any server operated by its author.

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, transfer or disclose your Google user data to any third party, and we do not use it to serve advertisements. No humans read your Google user data, except where you ask for support and explicitly provide it, or where required by law.

## 5. Limited Use

Gleaner's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 6. YouTube API Services

Gleaner uses YouTube API Services. By using Gleaner you agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's use of information is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

## 7. Retention and deletion

- **Disconnect:** you can disconnect your Google account in the app at any time. This deletes the stored refresh token and the cached subscription list from the server.
- **Revoke access:** you can also revoke Gleaner's access in your Google Account at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>, or via the [Google security settings page](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). After revocation, Gleaner can no longer read your subscriptions.
- **Delete your content:** transcripts and articles are files on your own server, and you can delete them at any time.

## 8. Security

Credentials are kept out of version control and stored with restricted file permissions on the server. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we recommend running Gleaner only on a machine you control.

## 9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
